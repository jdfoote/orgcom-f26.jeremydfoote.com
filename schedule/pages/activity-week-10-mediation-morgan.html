Your Role: Morgan — Content Manager

Do not share this with Jordan or Taylor before the roleplay begins.

Your situation: You have covered the 8 am Friday call six times in the last two months — far more than your fair share. You have a 45-minute commute and being in the office by 8 am on Fridays is genuinely hard. You never said anything directly to Taylor because you didn’t know why it was happening and you didn’t want to seem petty. But last week you finally went to Jordan.

Your goal: You want the rotation to go back to being fair. You don’t want to get Taylor in trouble — you just want the extra burden off your plate.

How you’re feeling

  • Frustrated that this has gone on for two months with no explanation.
  • A little awkward — you went to your supervisor without talking to Taylor first, and now you’re in a room together.
  • Willing to be reasonable, but only if Taylor actually acknowledges the situation has been unfair.

What you’re willing to do

  • Accept a temporary arrangement if there’s a real end date and a clear makeup plan.
  • Let the past two months go if things are fixed going forward.
  • Hear Taylor out — you genuinely don’t know what’s been going on.

What you don’t want

  • A vague promise that things will “even out eventually” with no specifics.
  • To feel like you’re the bad guy for raising this.
  • The status quo to continue indefinitely.
